# tasteskill v2 (Anti-Slop Frontend Design)

A page built by a model usually looks like the average of every page the model has seen: a centred headline over three lines, a little label above it, a row of icon cards, a soft gradient, a few thin rules and a monospace caption. Nothing in it is wrong and nothing in it was decided.

This skill replaces those defaults with intentional design decisions:
1. **Palette & Proportions**: Use one dominant ground (60%), one structural ink/secondary (30%), and one primary accent (10%) taken from the subject's world.
2. **House Style with No Ornaments**: Avoid stock AI card grids, meaningless pill badges, soft gradients, and rainbow status saturation.
3. **Typography & Substance**: Clear hierarchy, high contrast, scannable tabular numbers (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`), and real functional layouts that work on both desktop and mobile thumb reach.
